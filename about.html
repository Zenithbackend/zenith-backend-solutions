An accounting team built on one non-negotiable: reviewed quality.

Zenith Backend Solutions exists to give growing Canadian businesses and CPA firms the accounting capacity they need, without the hiring risk, management overhead, or quality gaps that come with scaling too fast.

Why we exist

Growth shouldn't mean lower quality.

Most growing businesses and firms hit the same wall: demand outpaces capacity before there's budget for another full-time hire. Overworking the team, rushing the work or turning clients away all cost something you don't get back. Zenith is the third option — a CPA-supervised backend team that plugs into your books, or your firm's white-label pipeline, with every file reviewed before it goes out the door.

Our team

Two teams, one standard.

A team of CPAs

Licensed CPAs review and sign off every file before it reaches you, and are on hand to answer the technical questions.

A team of experienced accountants
