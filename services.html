Bookkeeping

Daily transaction coding, reconciliations and clean books — on QuickBooks Online, Xero, Sage or our own in-house platform.

  • Daily and weekly transaction coding across bank, credit card and loan accounts, sized to your volume — from 50 to 5,000+ transactions a month.
  • Bank and credit card reconciliations closed within 3–5 business days of month-end, not caught up at year-end.
  • Accounts payable management: vendor bill entry, approval routing and payment runs, plus accounts receivable invoicing and aging follow-up.
  • Payroll journal entries and payroll account reconciliations for weekly, bi-weekly, semi-monthly or monthly cycles.
  • Clean, audit-ready books maintained on QuickBooks Online, Xero or Sage — or migrated onto our own in-house accounting software if you'd rather have one team building and running your books.
Where it fits

Books that are current every month, so reports, lenders and your accountant are never waiting on catch-up work — on whichever platform you already run, or ours.

Financial Reporting

Month-end packages, management reports and KPI dashboards, delivered within 5 business days of close.

  • Month-end and year-end close support run against a documented, checklist-driven close calendar.
  • Management reports and KPI dashboards covering gross margin, cash runway, AR/AP aging, burn rate and the metrics specific to your business.
  • Consolidated reporting across multiple entities, locations or franchise units — from a two-entity structure up to 20+.
  • Custom report packages formatted to your existing template, or built fresh in Excel, Google Sheets, Power BI, or our in-house reporting dashboard.
Where it fits

A dependable monthly rhythm of close, package and review, so you see performance while it still matters — not six weeks later.

Advisory Support

13-week cash-flow forecasts, 3-statement models and variance analysis to support real advisory conversations.

  • Rolling 13-week cash-flow forecasts and longer-range scenario modelling (best case, base case, worst case).
  • Monthly budget-to-actual analysis with variance commentary, not just a spreadsheet of numbers.
  • 3-statement financial models (P&L, balance sheet, cash flow) to support lending applications, capital raises or valuation conversations.
  • Board- and lender-ready supporting materials, built in Excel or in our in-house planning tool and formatted for external review.
Where it fits

Forecasts and models built for lenders, investors or your board — grounded in books we already know are accurate.

Tax Support

Year-round reconciliation and clean-up work, so your books are filing-ready well before any deadline.

  • Year-end working papers and supporting schedules prepared and indexed for your accountant, ahead of T2/T1 filing deadlines.
  • Quarterly or annual GST/HST reconciliations, and payroll remittance (T4/T4A) reconciliations, tied out to filed amounts.
  • Trial balance clean-up and account reclassification completed ahead of your filing window, not scrambled together at the deadline.
  • Organized, indexed records ready to support any CRA review or correspondence.
Where it fits

Records organized and reconciled year-round, so your own accountant or tax preparer can file with confidence. We don't file returns on your behalf.

Compilations & Reviews

Year-end financial statements prepared for your lender, investors or accountant, and CPA-reviewed before they reach you.

  • Financial statements prepared for banks, lenders, investors and shareholders, in the format your reader expects.
  • Compilation (Notice to Reader) and review engagement files prepared to CSRS/CSRE standard, ready for your CPA firm to issue.
  • Supporting schedules and working papers, indexed and cross-referenced so a reviewing CPA can move through the file quickly.
  • Every file reviewed and signed off by our in-house licensed CPAs before it's sent onward.
Where it fits

Statements your bank, lender or investors can rely on, prepared by our team and reviewed by a licensed CPA before you receive them.

Audit & Assurance

Full audit engagements, planned and tested under Canadian Auditing Standards, and signed off by our own in-house licensed CPAs.

  • Audit engagements planned, staffed and led directly by our in-house licensed CPAs — from single-entity owner-managed businesses to multi-entity groups.
  • Fieldwork, sampling, substantive testing and control walkthroughs carried out end-to-end by our team.
  • Working papers, PBC lists and supporting schedules prepared, cross-referenced and reviewed in-house before sign-off.
  • Engagement report reviewed and signed off by a licensed CPA before it reaches you.
Where it fits

Businesses and CPA firms that need a full audit, not just audit-ready books, carried out and signed off end-to-end by our own licensed CPAs.

Custom Accounting Software

Our own in-house accounting platform — used as-is, or customized around how your business or firm actually works.

  • A proprietary bookkeeping and reporting platform built in-house by our own engineering team, not a resold or white-labelled QuickBooks or Xero.
  • Custom modules built for your workflow: approval chains, industry-specific chart of accounts, and report templates matched to what your bank, board or partners expect.
  • Integrations with your existing bank feeds, POS, payroll or CRM systems, so data flows in without manual re-entry.
  • Runs alongside QuickBooks Online, Xero or Sage — keep your current software and layer our platform on top for the reporting it can't do, or migrate fully.
  • Supported and updated directly by the team that built it, so change requests don't sit in a third-party vendor's queue.
Where it fits

Businesses and CPA firms that have outgrown off-the-shelf software, or want automation, integrations and reporting that QuickBooks and Xero don't offer out of the box — built and customized to your workflow, not the other way around.
