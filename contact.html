FAQ

Common questions.

For businesses

Do you work directly with businesses, not just accounting firms?

Yes. Businesses across Canada engage Zenith directly as an outsourced accounting department for bookkeeping, reporting and advisory support. See the Canadian Businesses section for details.

Do you file taxes or GST/HST returns for Canadian businesses?

No. We prepare and organize the underlying bookkeeping so your business's own accountant or tax preparer can file with confidence — we don't file returns on a client's behalf.

Which accounting software do you support?

Our workflows are built primarily around QuickBooks Online, with experience across other major platforms used by accounting firms and businesses globally.

Can we start with only one client or a single set of books?

Yes. Many firms and businesses start with a single file during the trial period before expanding scope.

Security and getting started

How secure is client data?

We follow data privacy-aligned practices, use secure portal access, and back every engagement with confidentiality and non-disclosure agreements.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes. Every engagement includes confidentiality and non-solicitation agreements as standard practice.

How quickly can we get started?

Most firms and businesses move from discovery call to trial setup within one to two weeks.

For CPA and accounting firms

How does white-label bookkeeping work for firms?

We work behind your firm's brand. Files, reports, and communications are delivered under your name — your clients never see Zenith directly.

Will you communicate with my firm's clients?

No. All client-facing communication stays with your firm. We operate strictly as your backend team.

Do you prepare compilations, reviews, or audits?

Yes. Our in-house licensed CPAs prepare, test, and sign off on compilation, review, and audit engagement files directly — from working papers through to the final engagement report.
